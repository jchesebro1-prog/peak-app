# Peak Consulting Letter — Anatomy & Defaults

Source: `2026-07-13-fall-creek-hs-design-fee-proposal-rev1.pdf` (Fall Creek HS
gymnatorium design-fee proposal, REV 1, 2026-07-13; supplied by Jeff
2026-07-25). This is the format guide for the Consulting proposal builder —
closes homework item 1 of the 2026-07-25 remaining-items spec and answers
"#35-B: what's the default assumptions set" from the real letter.

## Document anatomy (maps onto the proposal-builder decisions)

1. **Date.**
2. **Recipient = the architect firm** (Bray Associates Architects, attn a
   named contact, street address). Confirms the engagement's architect link:
   the proposal is *addressed to* the architect, not the owner.
3. **Salutation** — "Dear <first name>:".
4. **Opening paragraph** — "Thank you for the opportunity to provide
   consulting services for the upcoming <project>. The following services
   were requested:"
5. **Primary scope bullet** — design, layout, construction drawings, and
   specifications for the covered systems (AV / theatrical lighting-rigging /
   curtains), plus coordination with the architect and other consultants.
6. **"Additionally, the scope of work shall include the following:"** — the
   standard-inclusions list. THIS is the checkable-assumptions section (see
   defaults below).
7. **Fee lines: one per scope, `<Scope title>: $<fee>`** — e.g.
   "Gymnatorium Audio-Video: $12,000.00" / "Gymnatorium
   Lighting/Rigging/Curtains: $18,000.00". Matches the decided scope shape
   (title + description + fee each; proposal total = sum of scope fees).
8. **Closing** — "We look forward to working with you on this project!" /
   "Respectfully," / signer name + title (here: Jason W. Keagy, Design and
   Operations).
9. **Footer** — PO Box 126, Reedsburg WI 53959 · info@peaksystemsgroup.com ·
   www.peaksystemsgroup.com · 608-524-1268 · fax 608-524-1269.

## Default assumptions checklist (seed set, Settings-editable)

Parameterized from the letter's standard-inclusions list — bracketed numbers
become per-proposal editable counts:

1. Participation in [2] in-person design review meetings with the owner, if
   necessary.
2. Participation in no more than [1] virtual meeting per month with the
   architect, engineering consultants, and the owner for design and
   coordination throughout the project.
3. Necessary drawing backgrounds shall be provided by others in AutoCAD .dwg
   format. Revit modeling will not be supported.
4. Provide a minimum of [2] progress drawing sets during design, in addition
   to the final construction documents.
5. Conduct shop drawing and submittal reviews during construction
   administration.
6. Conduct [1] site visit to observe work in progress during construction.
7. Conduct [1] site visit for final observations and punch list near the
   completion of construction.

## Notes for the builder

- Scope titles carry a venue/room prefix ("Gymnatorium …") — scope title
  should be free text, not a picklist.
- The letter has no separate exceptions section; exceptions land as unchecked
  assumptions or the shared exceptions list (per the 2026-07-25 spec §4).
- Assumption 3 (backgrounds by others / no Revit) doubles as an exception in
  spirit — keep it in the default set.
- Signer varies by job (Jason here) — signer picker, standard office
  numbers per D87 item 9-D.
