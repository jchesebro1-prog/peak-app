A. RIGGING PACKAGED HOISTS
  1. Basis of Design: Prodigy P1 as manufactured by ETC.
    a. Minimum Safety Factor: 10:1 for equipment and hardware unless specified otherwise.
    b. Standards Compliance: UL Listed and tested as a complete system made up of UL listed components.
    c. Hoists:
      1) Wire Rope Lift Lines: Design factor of 10:1. Ultimate strength: 4200 lb.
      2) Load Path Components Between Building Structure and Batten: Must exceed breaking strength of wire rope.
      3) Motor Brake: Rated at 125 percent of motor torque.
      4) Capable of Supporting the Following Loads:
        a) General Purpose Hoists:
          (1) P1900G 30 fpm (0.15 m per s): Capacity: 2000 lb (907 kg) in standard configuration. Powerhead (HxWxL): 16 x 18 x 53.5 inches (406 x 157 x 1359 mm). Weight: 580 lb (263 kg).
        b) Stage Electric Hoists:
          (1) P1500E 30 fpm (0.15 m per s): Capacity: 1500 lb (680 kg) in standard configuration. Powerhead (HxWxL): 16 x 18 x 53.5 inches (406 x 157 x 1359 mm). Weight: 580 lb (263 kg).
      5) Hoists: Major components.
        a) Powerhead.
        b) Compression tube with beam clamps, loft blocks, lift line and lift line terminations, Right Angle Cable Adjuster (RACA).
        c) Pipe batten.
        d) Electric Hoists: Major components.
          (1) Cable management system.
          (2) Power and control distribution strip.
      6) Features:
        a) Compression Tube: Prevents hoist system lateral forces from transferring to building. Hoisting systems imposing lateral loads on buildings are not acceptable.
        b) Built-in load cell.
        c) Built-in slack line sensor.
        d) Emergency contactor built into the hoist.
        e) Absolute position encoders.
    d. Compression Tube and Beam Clamps:
      1) Compression Tube: Extruded aluminum channel, engineered in conjunction with beam clamps to neutralize rigging-generated lateral forces on buildings.
        a) Support the system loft blocks.
        b) Sections joined in a continuous assembly by a pair of dedicated splicing plates at each tube joint.
        c) Installed with dedicated beam clamps that allow tube to snap into place and fractionally move horizontally under load.
      2) Beam Clamps: Capable of attaching to horizontal beams, joists, truss flanges or flat steel plates. Thickness: 1/4 to 1 inch (6 to 25 mm). Width: 4 to 14 inches (102 to 356 mm).
    e. Loft Blocks: Assemblies of steel side plates, a wire rope idler, sheave, bearings, shaft locked against rotation and support hardware.
      1) Loft blocks are inserted into the slot on the bottom of compression tube positioned no closer than 48 inches (1219 mm) from each other, unless muled.
      2) Loft Block Sheaves: 5 inches (127 mm) diameter. Contain a pair of press fit sealed ball bearings and are concentric about the hub and evenly balanced for rotation.
        a) Lift Lines: Travel in groove sized for 3/16 inch (5 mm) diameter wire rope per latest edition of Wire Rope Users' Manual, published by the Wire Rope Technical Board.
      3) Idler: on top of loft block to guide and support lift lines as they pass the block.
      4) Hoisting systems requiring loft blocks be mounted directly to facility structure are not acceptable.
