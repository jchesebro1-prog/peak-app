A. ENTERTAINMENT LUMINAIRES MOVING
  1. Basis of Design: Lonestar Prime as manufactured by ETC High End Systems.
  2. Standards Compliance:
    a. cETLus Listed.
    b. Listed to UL 1573.
    c. Certified to CSA C22.2 No. 166.
    d. CE Compliant.
    e. UKCA Compliant.
    f. CB Certified.
    g. RoHS Compliant.
  3. Source:
    a. Engine: Extra Bright Color Boost white LED engine.
    b. LED Power: 350 W.
    c. Maximum Integrating Sphere Output: 17,000 lumens.
    d. Native Color Temperature: 6500 K.
  4. Color:
    a. Color Mixing: Cyan, magenta, yellow, and CTO.
    b. Variable Color Temperature: Down to 2700 K.
    c. Color Wheel: Ten dichroic colors plus open.
  5. Optical:
    a. Beam Angle Range: 3.8 to 55 degrees.
    b. Pattern Projection:
      1) Rotating and indexable wheel with nine interchangeable patterns plus open.
      2) Fixed wheel with ten fixed patterns plus open.
    c. Animation: Bi-directional rotating animation wheel with linear insertion and variable angle.
    d. Prism:
      1) Five-facet star rotating prism.
      2) Four-facet linear rotating prism.
      3) Simultaneous prism insertion.
    e. Diffusion:
      1) Light and medium diffusion with variable insertion.
      2) No effect on focus or zoom.
    f. Iris: 16-blade iris.
    g. Framing: Four-plane, full-curtain framing system with 180-degree module rotation.
  6. Control:
    a. Input Method:
      1) DMX via 5-pin connector.
      2) Ethernet.
    b. Protocols:
      1) DMX via RS-485.
      2) Art-Net or sACN via Ethernet.
      3) RDM.
    c. Dimming: 16-bit, DMX controlled.
    d. Configuration:
      1) Full-color graphical user interface.
      2) Local control.
      3) NFC configuration.
  7. Physical:
    a. Pan Range: Selectable 630- or 540-degree range.
    b. Tilt Range: 260 degrees.
    c. Maximum Movement Speed:
      1) Pan: 360 degrees in 1.62 seconds.
      2) Tilt: 180 degrees in 0.88 seconds.
    d. Mounting Orientation: Any orientation.
    e. Environmental Rating: IP54 for indoor and temporary outdoor installations.
    f. Operating Temperature: Minus 10 to 35 degrees C (14 to 95 degrees F).
    g. Weight: 51 lb (23.1 kg), excluding mounting hardware.
  8. Electrical:
    a. Voltage Range: 100-240 V, 50-60 Hz.
    b. Input Method: REAN power X Series connector.
    c. Power Consumption: 550 W.
    d. Fixtures per Circuit:
      1) Three fixtures on an R20 module or similar circuit.
  9. Included Accessories:
    a. X Series IP65-to-bare-end fixture tail.
    b. Two 107 mm single-hole Omega brackets.
    c. Safety cable.
  10. RigPOV Configuration:
    a. LS-P-CAM: Provide Lonestar Prime with RigPOV camera configuration.
      1) Camera: 1080p low-latency camera.
      2) Communication: NDI HX, RTSP, RTMP, and SRT.
      3) Connection: RJ45 connection replacing the Ethernet thru connection.
      4) Environmental Rating: IP54 when installed.
