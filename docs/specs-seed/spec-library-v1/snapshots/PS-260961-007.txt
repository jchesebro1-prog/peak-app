A. ARCHITECTURAL CONTROL BUTTON STATIONS
  1. General
    a. The Lighting Control Stations shall be the Heritage Series Control Stations as manufactured by Electronic Theatre Controls, Inc., or equal.
    b. Functional
      1) The Unison Paradigm Control System shall be designed to allow control of lighting and associated systems via Button, Button/Fader, and Interface or Astronomical time clock controls. System shall allow the programming of presets, sequences, macros and time clock events.
      2) Station Button, Button/Fader, and Interface) control components shall be designed to operate standard default or custom system functions. Components shall operate default functions unless re-assigned via LightDesigner, the Windows-based configuration program.
      3) Stations (Button and Button/Fader) shall allow programming of station and component electronic lockout levels via LightDesigner.
    c. Mechanical
      1) Mounting: Flush-mount in industry-standard gang boxes (by others).
      2) Configuration: 1-gang button stations (1, 2, 5, 7, or 10 buttons) and keyswitch stations (maintained or momentary, with 0, 1, 2, or 5 buttons), legends as scheduled [per Drawings].
      3) Finish: [Cream (RAL 9001), Ivory (RAL 1015), Gray (RAL 7001), Black (RAL 9004), or Signal White (RAL 9003)], as selected by the Architect.
